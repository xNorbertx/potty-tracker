## App name

Potty Tracker

## Short description

A shared poop diary for your little one. Log, look back and stay in sync.

## Full description

When was the last poop? Keep the answer in one shared place.

Potty Tracker helps parents and caregivers keep a simple poop diary for each
child. From nappies to potty-training days, record what happened and look back
when you need to.

LOG THE DETAILS
Record the time and choose from five illustrated poop consistencies. Add size,
colour and a note when useful.

LOOK BACK EASILY
See entries on a calendar, jump to an earlier month or year, and return to today
with a tap.

SHARE THE DIARY
Invite another caregiver so everyone can see and update the same diary. Keep
separate diaries for multiple children and switch between them from the title.
Email verification is required to send invitations.

CELEBRATE STREAKS
Earn badges for 7, 14 and 30 consecutive days with a poop recorded. Achievements
belong to each child's diary and are shared with their caregivers. Badges reflect
your records, not a medical assessment or a target for your child's bowel habits.

KEEP A COPY
Export a diary as a PDF to save or share when you choose.

Free to use, with no advertisements or in-app purchases. Available in English.
An account is required; internet access is needed to sign in and synchronise
diaries between caregivers.

Potty Tracker is a record-keeping app for adult caregivers, not a medical device.
It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. Consult a
qualified healthcare professional for medical advice or concerns about your
child's health. It records poops; it does not provide a potty-training programme,
pee tracking or training reminders.

Questions? support@potty-tracker.com

Potty Tracker

Delete your account

You can delete your Potty Tracker account in Account settings → Delete account, or request deletion by email without opening the app.

Request deletion by email

Email support@potty-tracker.com with the subject Delete my Potty Tracker account. Send it from the address you use for your account, and say that you want your account and associated data deleted. Never send your password.

Request account deletion

If you no longer have access to that email address, contact support and explain this. Norbert Bakker, the app's operator, will help verify ownership before deleting anything. You do not need to reinstall the app.

What happens to your data

  • Your account, caregiver profile, verification records and invitations are deleted.
  • Your membership and recorded author details are removed from shared diaries. Entries and notes remain for the other caregivers.
  • If you are a diary's last caregiver, that diary, its entries and achievements are deleted.
  • Previously exported copies are not deleted. Personal information typed into retained notes is not automatically removed; mention it in your request if you want help removing it.

Deletion is permanent. Automated database cleanup may take time after your account is deleted. Email requests require an ownership check; support will confirm when your request has been handled. A temporary account-identifier record protects against reuse of old sign-in tokens, expires after two hours and is then automatically removed. Provider logs, delivery records, backups and support correspondence can have separate retention periods, as explained in the privacy policy.

Delete specific data instead

You can delete individual entries in the app without deleting your account. For another data-removal request, email support with the details you want removed.

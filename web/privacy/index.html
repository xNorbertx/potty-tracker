Privacy policy

Potty Tracker is operated by Norbert Bakker as an individual. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@potty-tracker.com.

The app is for adult parents and caregivers to keep a child's poop diary. It is not designed for children to create accounts. Only record information about a child you are authorised to care for, and only invite caregivers who should have access.

Information we process

  • Your account: email address, caregiver name, account identifier, sign-in provider and email-verification information. Firebase Authentication handles passwords and sign-in credentials. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, that provider supplies account information such as your identifier, email and profile name.
  • Diaries: the name you give each child, caregiver membership, invitations, and poop entries including dates, times, consistency, optional size, colour and notes. These entries may contain health information. Streak achievements are calculated from diary entries, and the app records which achievements have been celebrated.
  • Technical information: our hosting and authentication providers process connection and security information, including IP addresses, request information, SDK/platform versions and identifiers used to deliver their services. Firebase callable functions may also process a messaging token when one is available. Potty Tracker does not use it to send push notifications.
  • Support: your email address, message and any information you choose to send when you contact us. Please avoid sending unnecessary information about a child.

How we use information

We use account information to sign you in, identify caregivers, verify your email and manage invitations. We use diary information to store and display entries, synchronise them between devices and caregivers, calculate streaks and create exports you request. Technical information supports service delivery, security and troubleshooting. Support messages let us answer questions and handle privacy requests.

We do not sell your personal information or use diary information for advertising. The app has no advertising SDK, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics integration.

Who can access information

Other caregivers in a diary can see its entries and caregiver details, including names and email addresses. Joining or inviting someone gives them access to that diary's existing history as well as future entries. Caregivers can edit or delete entries and export the diary. Share invitation codes only with people you trust.

We use Google Firebase for authentication, database storage and backend processing; Resend for welcome and verification emails; Zoho Mail for support correspondence; and GitHub Pages for the web app and these public pages. These providers process information needed to provide their services. Google or Microsoft also processes your sign-in when you choose that option. The operator may access relevant information when necessary to resolve support, security or deletion requests.

A PDF export is created on your device. You decide whether and where to save, print or share it. Copies held by recipients are outside our control.

Storage and security

Connections to our backend use encryption in transit. Database access rules restrict diaries to their caregivers. This is not end-to-end encryption: authorised service providers and the operator can process data to operate the service.

Service providers may process information in countries outside your own, including the United States. Their applicable data-processing terms and international-transfer safeguards govern that processing. The app can cache diary data locally; protect access to your device and remove the app's stored data if you no longer want those local copies.

Retention and deletion

Account and diary information is kept while the account or shared diary remains active, unless you delete it. You can delete individual entries, delete a diary when you are its only caregiver, or delete your account in Account settings.

Account deletion removes your sign-in account, caregiver profile, verification records and personal invitations. It removes your membership details and recorded author name, email and account identifier from retained diaries, including diaries you previously left. If no caregivers remain, the diary and its entries and achievements are deleted. Cleanup runs on the server and is retried if interrupted; it may not be instantaneous.

Shared diaries remain available to their other caregivers. Their entries and notes remain, with your account attribution removed. Information someone typed into a note is not automatically identified or erased; contact us if a retained note contains personal information you want removed. Deleting your account does not delete another caregiver's account or copies they have already exported.

A temporary security record containing the deleted account identifier blocks reuse of old sign-in tokens. It expires after two hours and is removed by the database's automatic expiration process, which is not instantaneous. Provider security logs, email delivery records and backups can follow separate retention schedules; deletion from the live diary does not instantly erase those records. Contact us about records retained by providers or in support correspondence.

You can also request account and data deletion without the app. We verify ownership before acting on an email request. Support correspondence is used to handle your request and may be retained where needed to document its resolution or meet legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can correct your caregiver name, edit or delete entries, leave a shared diary, export a diary and delete your account. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, erase or receive your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact support@potty-tracker.com to exercise these rights or ask about a child's information. You may complain to your local data-protection authority.

We will update this page when our data practices change. Material changes will also be communicated through the app or email where appropriate.
